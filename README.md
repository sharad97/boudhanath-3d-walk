# Boudhanath & the Kathmandu Valley — 3D walk, ride, drive & fly

Walk the kora around Boudhanath Stupa (Kathmandu, Nepal), ride a bicycle or motorbike, or drive a car through the surrounding streets, in your browser. Works on iPhone and iPad (Safari) and on desktop.

**Play:** https://sharad97.github.io/boudhanath-3d-walk/ — the latest: the Boudha walk inside a 30 km circle of the whole Kathmandu Valley (~12.8 MB page).

**Earlier Boudha-only version (for comparison):** https://sharad97.github.io/boudhanath-3d-walk/boudha-classic/

The valley adds 672,000 OpenStreetMap buildings (facades from the same procedural atlas as Boudha), 66,000 roads, rivers you can swim in, OSM 3D temple parts and hand-built landmarks (Durbar Squares, Swayambhunath, Pashupatinath, Dharahara, Changu Narayan, Narayanhiti, Dakshinkali), traffic and people, disasters anywhere, a helicopter, and an airplane that starts on Tribhuvan's runway 02. **Go to…** jumps to any place.
- Terrain: Copernicus DEM GLO-30 © DLR e.V. 2010-2014 and © Airbus Defence and Space GmbH 2014-2018, provided under COPERNICUS by the European Union and ESA.
- Building heights without an OSM tag: GHS-BUILT-H 2018 (Global Human Settlement Layer), © European Union, CC BY 4.0 — calibrated against OSM-tagged heights.

- Layout, building footprints and streets: © [OpenStreetMap contributors](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright), ODbL.
- Building looks were matched by eye to Google Street View; no Google imagery is used or included. Every texture is generated in the browser.
- Built with [three.js](https://threejs.org) (MIT), inlined so the page is a single file.

Controls: drag to look; W A S D / arrows or the on-screen joystick to move. **Drive** gets you a car, motorbike or bicycle (F to get in/out). **Disasters** adds weather and events. On iPhone, Share → Add to Home Screen runs it full screen.
