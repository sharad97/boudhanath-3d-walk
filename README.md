# Boudhanath Stupa — 3D walk, ride & drive

Walk the kora around Boudhanath Stupa (Kathmandu, Nepal), ride a bicycle or motorbike, or drive a car through the surrounding streets, in your browser. Works on iPhone and iPad (Safari) and on desktop.

**Play:** https://sharad97.github.io/boudhanath-3d-walk/

- Layout, building footprints and streets: © [OpenStreetMap contributors](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright), ODbL.
- Building looks were matched by eye to Google Street View; no Google imagery is used or included. Every texture is generated in the browser.
- Built with [three.js](https://threejs.org) (MIT), inlined so the page is a single file.

Controls: drag to look; W A S D / arrows or the on-screen joystick to move. **Drive** gets you a car, motorbike or bicycle (F to get in/out). **Disasters** adds weather and events. On iPhone, Share → Add to Home Screen runs it full screen.
